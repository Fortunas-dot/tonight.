Last updated: [DATE] · Applies to the Tonight app for iPhone

The short version. Tonight is a members-only app for adults. We collect what the app needs to work: your profile, your photos, your messages and your safety settings. You can delete your account and everything in it from Settings at any time.

4. Who can see what

  • Other members see your profile once your membership is accepted. Your city can be hidden (they then see “Nearby”). Blurred photos stay blurred until you match.
  • Your matches see your messages to them.
  • Lounge posts are shown under your alias, not your name.
  • Our team sees applications and reported content to review them, and nothing else unless needed for safety or the law.
  • Your email address is never shown to anyone.

5. How long we keep it

  • Your account data: as long as you have an account. When you delete your account (Settings › Delete my account), we delete your profile, photos, messages, likes, matches, posts and settings straight away.
  • Face check selfie: deleted as soon as the check is done.
  • Backups: removed in the regular backup cycle, at most [30] days later.
  • If the law requires us to keep something (for example after a police request), we keep only that, only as long as needed.

6. Your rights

You can ask to see, correct, export or delete your data, object to processing based on legitimate interest, and withdraw consent at any time (this doesn’t affect what happened before). Most of this you can do in the app yourself. For the rest, email info@fortunas.nl; we answer within one month. You can also complain to the Dutch Data Protection Authority (Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens).

7. Security

Data is encrypted in transit, access is limited per member by database rules, photos are only reachable through short-lived links, and you can lock the app with Face ID. No system is perfect; if a breach affects you, we’ll tell you and the authority as the law requires.

8. Adults only

Tonight is for people aged 18 and over. We don’t knowingly collect data from anyone younger and remove such accounts when we find them.

9. Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we’ll tell you in the app before it takes effect.
